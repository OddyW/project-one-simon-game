Game Info

How to Play

Press PLAY and watch the tiles flash. When the message says "Your Turn!", click the tiles in the same order. Every round adds one more tile to the sequence. One wrong tile and the game is over.

Buttons

  • PLAY / Restart: starts a game. It restarts the game on your turn, and it is greyed out while Simon is playing.
  • Easy / Medium / Hard: pick before you start. Harder levels need more rounds to win and speed up faster. They are locked during a game.
  • Clear Highscore: resets your saved best score.
  • Mute: turns the sounds off and on.

Scores

Your score is the number of rounds you have cleared. Your high score is saved in this browser.
